⚙️ AI Engine Settings

You must have at least one AI model configured and selected to run the app.
The app is arranged so usage should not meaningfully spend your AI credit limits.

Chrome Browser (window.LanguageModel)
Cost: Completely free — runs on your hardware
Setup: Open a new Chrome tab and type chrome://flags. Find the setting "Prompt API" and set it to Enabled.
